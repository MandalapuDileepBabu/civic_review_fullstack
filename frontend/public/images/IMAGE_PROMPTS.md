## How to use after generating

1. Save files to `frontend/public/images/` with the filenames above.
2. The app auto-loads local images via `frontend/src/data/images.js`.
3. Until files exist, Unsplash fallbacks are used automatically.

## Quick generation tools

- ChatGPT / DALL·E
- Midjourney
- Leonardo.ai
- Adobe Firefly

Use the prompts as-is or add: `--style raw --ar 16:9` (Midjourney) or equivalent in your tool.
